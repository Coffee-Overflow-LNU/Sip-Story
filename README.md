# Sip-Story

Every book deserves its own coffee.

Readspresso is an app for people who love reading with a cup of coffee. It lets you track the books you read, discover new ones by genre or plot description, suggests a coffee that matches each book, and shows cafés in Lviv where you can order it. You can also read books right inside the app.

🚧 Work in progress. A study project by team [Coffee Overflow].

✨ Features
📖 My Books
Lists: Read, Currently Reading, Want to Read
Ratings and reviews
Note which coffee you had with each book

☕ Coffee Pairing
Every book has a genre and a mood, and the app uses them to suggest a coffee:
Genre / Mood	Coffee
Detective, noir	Espresso, ristretto
Romance	Raf, lavender latte
Fantasy	Cinnamon latte
Thriller	Cold brew
Classics	Filter coffee, americano
Horror	Black coffee, Irish coffee

🗺️ Lviv Cafés
A map of cafés that serve the suggested coffee
Café details: address, menu, opening hours
🔍 Book Recommendations
By genre: filter books by genre and mood
By plot: describe the story you want to read in your own words, and the app finds similar books using AI

Example query: "something about a girl who moves to a small town and uncovers an old secret"

📱 Built-in Reader
Read EPUB books
Remembers where you stopped
Font and theme settings (light / dark / "coffee")
Public-domain books (Ukrainian classics) and your own uploaded files
